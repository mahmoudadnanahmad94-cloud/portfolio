Who we are

Steel, wooden and glass fire-rated doors, emergency exits and WPC doors — 20 to 180 minutes.

Platinum Fire Door is a UAE-based company specialized in providing fire-rated door solutions across the United Arab Emirates. We offer a comprehensive range of steel fire doors, wooden fire doors, fire-rated glass doors, double doors and emergency exit doors, designed to meet recognized safety and quality requirements — for villas, residential and commercial buildings and various facilities.

Certifications & standards

UL NFPA EN BS GB Intertek SGS

Solutions & Products

What we deliver

01

Steel fire doors

Single and double, fire-rated from 20 up to 180 minutes; anti-theft, soundproof and insulated.

02

Wooden fire doors

Timber fire-rated doors, 30 / 60 / 90 minutes, acoustic insulation 33–38 dB.

03

Fire-rated glass doors

Fire-resistant glazing 26, 30, 35 and 55 mm.

04

Emergency exit doors

Panic bars, vision panels, louvers and door closers.

05

WPC doors

Waterproof, anti-termite, eco-friendly interior doors in 18+ designs.

06

Custom sizes & finishes

Left/right hand, reverse swing, RAL colours and hardware to suit each project.

Are your doors certified?

Yes — certified to UL / NFPA / EN / BS / GB standards with Intertek and SGS test reports.
