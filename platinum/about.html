Which is a trusted elevator company in Abu Dhabi?

Platinum Elevator is a national elevator company with an office on Corniche Street, Abu Dhabi (3 Sails Tower). We supply, install and maintain home, villa, building, panoramic and service elevators using internationally certified products (TSE, BSI, TÜV, ISO, CE), with free consultation and site visits across Abu Dhabi, Khalifa City, Mussafah and Al Ain.

Do you install elevators in Dubai?

Yes. Our Dubai office in Business Bay (Bayswater Tower) handles elevator supply, installation and maintenance for villas, residential towers, offices and commercial buildings across Dubai — from Dubai Hills and Al Barsha to Deira, JVC and Dubai Marina.

Is there an elevator company near me in Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah or Fujairah?

Yes — Platinum serves every emirate: Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Al Ain, in addition to Abu Dhabi and Dubai. Our teams travel to your site for a free inspection, installation and scheduled maintenance, so you get the same service wherever you are in the UAE.

Do you also provide HVAC, CCTV, smart home and fire-rated doors in the UAE?

Yes. Besides elevators, Platinum has four more divisions: HVAC (VRF systems and chillers with Blue Star), smart home automation, security systems (CCTV, access control, turnstiles, intercoms) and certified fire-rated doors (20–180 minutes) — one company for all your building systems in Abu Dhabi, Dubai and the rest of the UAE.

Do you offer elevator maintenance and repair?

Yes — preventive maintenance contracts, repairs and modernisation for elevators in Abu Dhabi, Dubai and all emirates, carried out by our own technical team.

How do I get a quotation?

Use the Request a Quote form, call or WhatsApp us. A specialist will contact you, visit the site for free and send a clear quotation.
