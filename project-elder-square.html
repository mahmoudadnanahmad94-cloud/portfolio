Elder Square Clinic

Elder Square Clinic2024Jumeirah, Dubai

Overview

Complete IT infrastructure for a brand-new clinic built from scratch, from design coordination and site supervision to testing and handover.

Result

Delivered 15 days ahead of schedule despite site constraints caused by the 2024 flooding.

Leak-resistant piping

Selected pipe types suited to the conditions so the installation stayed leak-free — even through the 2024 floods.

Progress tracker for labour & engineers

Ran a progress tracker to allocate workers and engineers on site task by task. It cut wasted time between tasks, kept the work organised through Ramadan, and brought handover 15 days early.

  1. Structured cabling — 350 network cables
  2. Servers, Active Directory, switches and storage
  3. Ubiquiti network, firewall and UPS
  4. Linkus PABX with IP phones and nurse call
  5. BioTime access control and attendance, access–fire alarm integration
  6. CCTV across two floors and Bosch sound system
  7. Workstations, printing and Microsoft 365
